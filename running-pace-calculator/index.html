How is running pace calculated?

Pace = Total Time ÷ Distance. For example, if you run 5K in 25 minutes, your pace is 25 ÷ 5 = 5:00 per km, or 25 ÷ 3.10686 ≈ 8:03 per mile. This calculator handles all the conversions automatically.

What is negative splits running?

Negative splits means running the second half of a race faster than the first half. It's the most effective race strategy for most distances, helping you conserve energy early and finish strong. Most world records are set with negative or even splits.

How do I calculate my marathon pace from a 5K?

A rough formula: multiply your 5K time by 9 to estimate your marathon time. For example, a 25-minute 5K suggests a ~3:45 marathon. However, this assumes strong marathon-specific training (long runs, tempo runs). Use the pace found above to plan your marathon splits.

About This Running Pace Calculator

This running pace calculator solves the three core questions every runner faces: What pace do I need? (enter distance + goal time), How long will it take? (enter distance + pace), and How far did I run? (enter time + pace). It works for any distance — 5K, 10K, half marathon, marathon, or a custom run — in both miles and kilometers.

Unlike distance-specific tools, this calculator is built for everyday training decisions: figuring out your easy run pace, planning long-run targets, converting a training plan from one unit system to another, or verifying your Garmin watch data. Just switch modes with the tabs and enter what you know.
