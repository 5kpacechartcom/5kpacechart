How Running Pace Works

Pace vs. Speed

Pace is time per unit distance (min/km or min/mile). Speed is distance per unit time (km/h or mph). Runners use pace; cyclists use speed. Both measure the same thing from opposite angles.

Miles vs. Kilometers

1 mile = 1.60934 km. A 9:00/mile pace equals 5:35/km. Road races in the USA and UK use miles; most of the world uses km. Our tools output both so you always have both.

Even Splits vs. Negative Splits

Even splits means running every km at the same pace. Negative splits means running the second half slightly faster. Most PBs and BQ qualifications use negative splits — start conservative, finish strong.
