About 5kPaceChart.com

5kPaceChart.com is a free resource for runners who want clear, accurate pace information without the clutter. We built this site because finding a simple, well-designed 5K pace chart online was surprisingly difficult — most results were PDFs from local running clubs or blog posts buried in generic calculators.

What We Offer

5K Pace Chart

Interactive chart from 15–60 min with per-mile and per-km paces.

10K Pace Chart

Full 10K pace reference for every finish time from 30–90 min.

5K Pace Calculator

Convert between time and pace instantly.

Mile Pace Calculator

Convert min/mile to min/km and back.

Running Pace Calculator

Any distance: find pace, time, or distance.

Half Marathon Pace

Pace chart and calculator for 13.1 miles.

Our Approach

Every tool on this site loads instantly (no frameworks, no trackers beyond standard analytics), works on mobile, and gives you the answer in the fewest possible clicks. All calculations use precise distances: 5K = 5.000 km = 3.10686 miles, 10K = 10.000 km = 6.21371 miles, half marathon = 21.0975 km = 13.1094 miles.
